Our Story

Where smart farming meets Zambian pride

Bellson Farms was founded on a simple belief: that Zambia deserves access to the freshest, most nutritious food — grown right here at home, using intelligent farming practices that honour our land and our heritage.

We raise our chickens, source our fish, and grow our vegetables the smart way — ensuring every product is nutritious, sustainably produced, and deeply rooted in Zambian culture.

Modern techniques combined with traditional wisdom for better yields and healthier animals.

Meal Ideas

From our kitchen to yours

Quick, delicious recipes that make the most of our fresh Zambian produce.

Stir Fry Recipe Image
Quick Nkhuku Stir Fry
  1. 1 Heat 2 tbsp oil. Add sliced onion and bell peppers, sauté until softened.
  2. 2 Add chopped Nkhuku and 2 tsp soy sauce, stir fry until heated thoroughly.
  3. 3 Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of chilli flakes. Serve over rice.
Roast Chicken Recipe Image
Oven-Roasted Whole Nkhuku
  1. 1 Rub whole chicken with garlic, lemon, paprika, salt and olive oil.
  2. 2 Roast at 200°C for 1 hr 20 min, basting halfway through.
  3. 3 Rest 10 minutes before carving. Serve with roasted potatoes.
Bream Curry Recipe Image
Quick Bream Curry
  1. 1 Sauté onion, ginger, garlic, curry powder. Add tomatoes and simmer.
  2. 2 Cut Isabi into chunks, add to pan, cover and cook 5–7 minutes until flaky.
  3. 3 Stir in coconut milk and lemon juice. Serve hot over rice.
Veggie Sauté Recipe Image
Quick Ma Veggie Sauté
  1. 1 Heat 1 tbsp oil. Add sliced tomatoes, green peppers, and cucumbers. Sauté 5 min.
  2. 2 Season with salt, pepper, and herbs (oregano or basil).
  3. 3 Serve hot as a side dish or over a simple grain.
