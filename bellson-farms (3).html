Our Story

Where smart farming meets Zambian pride

Bellson Farms was founded on a simple belief: that Zambia deserves access to the freshest, most nutritious food — grown right here at home, using intelligent farming practices that honour our land and our heritage.

We raise our chickens, collect fresh eggs and grow our herbs the smart way, so every product is nutritious, sustainably produced, and deeply rooted in Zambian culture.

Meal Ideas

From our kitchen to yours

Quick, delicious recipes that make the most of our Nkhuku, eggs and fresh herbs.

Stir Fry
Quick Nkhuku Stir Fry
  1. 1 Heat 2 tbsp oil. Add sliced onion and bell peppers, sauté until softened.
  2. 2 Add chopped dressed Nkhuku and 2 tsp soy sauce, stir fry until cooked through.
  3. 3 Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of chilli flakes. Serve over rice.
Herb-Roasted Nkhuku
Herb-Roasted Whole Nkhuku
  1. 1 Rub a dressed Nkhuku with garlic, lemon, salt, olive oil and chopped rosemary and thyme.
  2. 2 Roast at 200°C for about 1 hr 20 min, basting halfway through.
  3. 3 Rest 10 minutes before carving. Serve with roasted potatoes.
Smoked Nkhuku & Herb Salad
Smoked Nkhuku & Herb Salad
  1. 1 Pull the smoked Nkhuku into strips, discarding the skin if you like.
  2. 2 Toss with chopped parsley, mint and dill, lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. 3 Serve on fresh greens or in bread rolls.
Farm Egg & Chive Omelette
Farm Egg & Chive Omelette
  1. 1 Whisk 3 eggs with salt, pepper and a splash of water.
  2. 2 Pour into a buttered pan over medium heat, stirring gently until just set.
  3. 3 Scatter chopped chives and parsley, fold, and serve warm.
